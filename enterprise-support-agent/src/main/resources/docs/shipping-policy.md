# Shipping Policy

**Standard shipping** takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $35.
Orders under $35 have a flat $4.99 shipping fee.

**Express shipping** takes 2-3 business days and costs $12.99, regardless of
order size.

**Order status definitions:**
- PROCESSING: the order has been received but not yet shipped.
- SHIPPED: the order has left the warehouse and is in transit.
- DELIVERED: the order has been delivered to the shipping address.
- DELAYED: the order is behind its expected shipping window, usually due to
  a warehouse inventory issue. Customers should be told the new expected
  ship date is typically 3-5 additional business days, and offered a
  discount code if they've been waiting more than 10 business days total.

**Lost packages:** if a package shows as DELIVERED but the customer says
they never received it, do not issue an automatic refund. This must be
escalated to a support agent to file a carrier claim first.

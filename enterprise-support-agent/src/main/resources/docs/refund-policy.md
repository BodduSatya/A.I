# Refund Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of the order date,
provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

**Refunds do NOT require a support agent's manual sign-off when:**
- The order is within the 30-day window, AND
- The order status is DELIVERED (not SHIPPED or PROCESSING), AND
- The refund amount is under $100.

**Refunds DO require a support agent's manual review when:**
- The order is outside the 30-day window (case-by-case exception approval).
- The refund amount is $100 or more.
- The customer reports the item as damaged or defective (may need photos
  before a refund is approved).
- The order was already marked REFUNDED once before (possible duplicate
  request).

Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically appear
within 5-10 business days after being issued.
